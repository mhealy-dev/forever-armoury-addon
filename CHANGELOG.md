# Changelog

## 0.6.0 (2026-10-25)
- First release: your talent builds, leveling gear and Legacy plans, in the game.
- On foreverarmoury.app, choose Copy for addon. Then type /fa import and paste.
- The next talent, gear piece or Legacy perk sits beside its window.
- Click a talent or perk to take it, or press Apply for all.

Something off? [Tell us on GitHub](https://github.com/mhealy-dev/forever-armoury-addon/issues).
