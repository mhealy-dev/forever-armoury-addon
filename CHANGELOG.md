# Changelog

## 0.6.0 (2026-10-25)

The first release. Forever Armoury puts your plans from [foreverarmoury.app](https://foreverarmoury.app) in the game. On the site, choose **Copy for addon**. In the game, type `/fa import` and paste.

- **Talents.** Your build sits beside the talent window with the next talent lit. Click it to take it, or press **Apply** to take every point that's due.
- **Leveling gear.** Your leveling route sits beside the character window: what to wear now and what's next, with where each piece drops. Every route is built in.
- **Legacy.** Your Legacy plan sits beside its window with the next perk lit. Press **Apply** to buy what's due across all three trees.

Install it with the CurseForge app, or unzip it into your AddOns folder as `ForeverArmoury`.

Commands: `/fa`, `/fa gear`, `/fa legacy`, `/fa import`, `/fa options`.

New, built on the Forever beta. Something off? [Tell us on GitHub](https://github.com/mhealy-dev/forever-armoury-addon/issues) with what `/fa debug` says.
