# Forever Armoury

Your plans from [foreverarmoury.app](https://foreverarmoury.app), in the game. Plan on the site, copy a code, paste it in, and see what to take, wear and buy next.

**Talents.** Your build beside the talent window: points taken, the next one lit, the rest with their levels. Hover to find it in the tree, click to take it, shift-click to spend every point. One click rebuilds a build after a trainer reset.

**Leveling gear.** The site's route beside the character window: what to wear at your level and what's coming, with where each piece drops. All routes ship with the addon.

**Legacy.** Your Legacy plan beside its window, perks in buying order, Apply across all three trees.

**How.** On the site, choose **Copy for addon**. In the game, `/fa import` and paste. Each part has a switch, and an **FA** button on each window hides the panel.

**Commands.** `/fa`, `/fa gear`, `/fa legacy`, `/fa import`, `/fa export`, `/fa apply`, `/fa options`, `/fa debug`.

New, built for the Forever beta. Something off? [Tell us on GitHub](https://github.com/mhealy-dev/forever-armoury-addon/issues) with what `/fa debug` says. Open source, MIT. A fan project, not connected with Blizzard Entertainment.
